Song Geometry Mapper

Version 1.3.0 • Geekatplay Studio

How it works

Song Geometry Mapper converts an audio track into frame-wise spectral descriptors and maps those frames into a 3D structure. Each node is one time slice of the song.

The 3D Construction

Analysis: We extract RMS, centroid, spread, rolloff, flatness, peak frequency, zero-crossing rate, and flux per frame.

Mapping: Choose Manifold (PCA) for structure clusters, Time Spine for chronology, Hybrid Flow for both, or Helix Orbit for spiral timeline motion. Similar sounding regions become spatially closer in manifold-style modes.

Observatory Layer: The Observatory preset adds a more analytical presentation with structural scan halos and orbit rings, while keeping the original geometry modes intact. It also loads a calmer camera and lower-noise FX profile when selected.

Cathedral Layer: The Cathedral preset adds vaulted arches, beacon spires, and a stronger stage silhouette without replacing the base graph. It also loads a more theatrical camera and glow profile when selected.

Analyzer Modes

Classic (Browser): drop audio and analyze locally in the browser.
Voice / Deep (Backend): drop one audio file, backend analyzer auto-generates geometry JSON, and loads it for playback sync.

Controls

  • Help on any control: Rest the pointer on a control for about half a second and a popup explains what it does and when to use it. Keyboard focus shows the same help; Esc dismisses it.
  • Playback Speed: Slow the transport to 0.05x for close observation, or speed it to 2x to skim. The geometry runs on the audio clock, so trails and ribbons keep their shape at every speed, and pausing freezes the trail for inspection. Use [ and ] to step, \ to return to 1x.
  • Mapping Mode: Switch among Manifold, Time Spine, Hybrid Flow, and Helix Orbit views.
  • Connections: Use temporal and similarity links, with straight or wave style.
  • Observatory Overlay: Add structural scan rings on top of the current map without replacing legacy presets or controls.
  • Cathedral Overlay: Add vaulted arches and beacon spires as another optional scene layer.
  • Scene Defaults: Selecting Observatory or Cathedral also loads recommended camera and FX defaults, and switching back to a legacy preset restores your prior non-scene settings.
  • Refine Geometry: Use song-aware auto spread or manual spread plus X/Y/Z offsets.
  • Visibility: Use "Nodes Only" to hide edges and effects for a cleaner view.
